Entra risky users

Kustodian1.2.0 · frodehus
Weekly review of risky users and their sign-ins.

What it answers

Which users Entra ID Protection currently rates as risky, how that changed over the week, and which of their sign-ins were flagged, side by side in one workbook.

How to use it

Open Weekly risky-user review from Saved. Pick a risk level and a time range; the table lists the users with their last risky sign-in.

Tick users and press Confirm compromised to mark them in Entra. The button runs the Azure CLI on your machine and always asks first.

Needs

Entra ID P2 for risk data, and Defender advanced hunting for the sign-in widget.
